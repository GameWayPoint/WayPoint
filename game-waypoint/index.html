FAQ

Good to know

How Game Waypoint works — and what it isn't.

Is this real navigation?
Yes. Game Waypoint uses real maps, your real GPS position and real routing — it simply reskins the look.
Which games are these styles from?
Original styles inspired by iconic open-world games, made with our own art and icons. Game Waypoint is independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with any game or publisher.
Does it work in Apple CarPlay?
That's the goal — a styled map on your car's screen. CarPlay navigation requires Apple's approval and a Premium subscription.
What does it cost?
Exploring and searching is free. Premium unlocks Driving Mode for about $4.99/month, localized to your region.
Do you track me?
No account required, and your location stays on your device. Privacy-first by design.
